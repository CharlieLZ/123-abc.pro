Accessibility

Local activities include keyboard focus, spoken prompts, captions, transcripts, and reduced-motion support.

Keyboard

Use Tab to move between controls. Arrow keys move between answer choices. Enter or Space activates the focused control, and Escape closes dialogs.

Captions and transcripts

Accessible videos include audio-aligned captions. Transcript buttons open local text and visual-description pages without leaving this site.

Motion and sound

New activities respect the device reduced-motion preference. Spoken prompts can be replayed with the music button, while video volume and captions use the player controls.
