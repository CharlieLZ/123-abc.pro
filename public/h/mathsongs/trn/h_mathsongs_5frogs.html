Five Little Frogs
Transcript & Visual Description

Audio Visual
Five Little Speckled Frogs.
(crickets chirping)
(frogs croaking)
(lively piano music)		 A green curtain with silhouettes of frogs rises. There are five frogs sitting on a log at night. The sun comes up. The frogs bob their heads up and down to the music.
Five little speckled frogs
Sat on a speckled log
Eating some most delicious bugs
Yum, yum!		 Dragonflies and other bugs fly around the frogs' heads. One of the frogs sticks his tongue out and eats the dragonfly. The frog smiles and rubs his belly.
One jumped into the pool
Where it was nice and cool
Then there were four green speckled frogs
Glub, glub!		 The frog jumps into a pond, making a splash, and swims away. Dragonflies fly by and shape the number 4.
Four little speckled frogs
Sat on a speckled log
Eating some most delicious bugs
Yum, yum!		 The four frogs eat flies that are buzzing around. The frogs all smile and lick their lips.
One jumped into the pool
Where it was nice and cool
Then there were three green speckled frogs
Glub, glub!		 One of the frogs jumps and does a cannonball into the pond. The other frogs smile and jump up and down on the log. A group of flies buzz in and shape the number 3.
Three little speckled frogs
Sat on a speckled log
Eating some most delicious bugs
Yum, yum!		 The three frogs eat water skipper bugs that are gliding across the pond. The middle frog smiles and rubs his belly.
One jumped into the pool
Where it was nice and cool
Then there were two green speckled frogs
Glub, glub!		 The middle frog jumps into the pond and swims away. Water skipper bugs swim on the pond and shape the number 2. The other two frogs jump up and down on the log.
Two little speckled frogs
Sat on a speckled log
Eating some most delicious bugs
Yum, yum!		 Both frogs bob their heads up and down. Butterflies fly around the frogs' heads. The frogs stick their tongues out and eat some butterflies. They smile and rub their bellies.
One jumped into the pool
Where it was nice and cool
Then there was one green speckled frog
Glub, glub!		 One of the frogs jumps into the pond and swims away. The last frog jumps and does front flips on the log. Butterflies fly in and shape the number 1.
One little speckled frog
Sat on a speckled log
Eating some most delicious bugs
Yum, yum!		 A beetle crawls across the log toward the frog. The last frog sticks his tongue out and eats the beetle. A second beetle crawls across the screen and the frog eats that one too.
One jumped into the pool
Where it was nice and cool
Then there were no green speckled frogs
Glub, glub!		 The last frog jumps and does a belly flop into the pond. The frog swims around, pokes his head above the water, and smiles.
THE END The curtains close.
