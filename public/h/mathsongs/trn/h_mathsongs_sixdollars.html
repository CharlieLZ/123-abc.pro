I Have Six Dollars.
(bouncy instrumental music)		 An orange curtain rises. Mox the Fox skips through a market holding some money.

Now I have four dollars to share with my friend		 He holds up six dollars, spins, and waves it around. Mox the Fox visits a purple booth at the market and buys a drink. He turns around and gives Tin Man a dollar to go spend. Mox the Fox holds up his remaining four dollars.
I have four dollars.
Yes, I have four dollars.

I lent another.
Now I have two dollars to share with my friend.		 He holds up four dollars, spins, and waves it around. Back at the market, Mox the Fox visits another booth and buys a bag of popcorn. He turns around and gives Zac the Rat a dollar to spend at the market.
I have two dollars.
Yes, I have two dollars.
I've never had so much money I could spend.
So I spent one dollar of it,
I lent another.
Now I have nothing to share with my friend.		 Mox the Fox holds up two dollars, spins, and waves it around. He goes back to the market and buys an ice cream cone. He turns around and gives Peg the Hen a dollar to spend.
I now have nothing.
Yes, I now have nothing.
With nothing, how can I share with my friend?
I have laughter,
And I have kindness.
And I have time that I'm happy to spend.		 Mox the Fox frowns and holds empty hands in the air. He kicks the dirt around him. Gus the Duck walks by. Mox the Fox tells Gus the Duck a joke and they both laugh. All of the friends come over and join in a group hug.
THE END The curtains close.
